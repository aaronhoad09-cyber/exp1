Experimenter console

Open with Shift+E or by triple-clicking the “Roulette” title. Participants never see this.

Session

Starting a session resets the money, the round counter and the results rail, then records every spin.

Back-to-back: starts recording the ID above (or P001 if it's empty). When that person finishes Part 2, the table counts down 5 seconds and starts the next ID automatically (P001 → P002 → P003…), skipping any ID already recorded on this device.

Google Sheet

Every spin is sent to the Sheet as it happens. If the connection drops, spins wait on this device and are sent automatically once it is back. Each participant gets their own tab, plus an All spins tab.

Game settings

Part 1 is the control: a fair 50/50 wheel. When it ends (goal reached, out of money, or maximum rounds) the table moves to Part 2 by itself with fresh starting money. Part 2 is assigned at random, 50/50, to land on red 85% of the time or on black 85% of the time. The session status above shows which one this participant got. Clicking box 1 or 2 on the table jumps straight to that part.

A session ends when the participant reaches the goal, runs out of money, or hits the maximum rounds.
